# Awesome Digital Gifts [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Ways to send something that isn't a card with a GIF in it.

A birthday arrives, you're 400km away, and a store-bought box won't make it. This list collects things you can actually send: pages, audio, video, games, time capsules, and the open-source templates to build your own.

Every entry is free or has a real free tier. No affiliate links.

## Interactive pages

Pages the recipient has to touch, not just scroll.

- [Gifts QR](https://giftsqr.com) — 34 fill-in templates (birthday matrix, star sky, voice wall, scratch card, mixtape), delivered as a link or printable QR. Free to create.
- [Kudoboard](https://www.kudoboard.com) — group card that collects messages from many people into one board. Free tier limited to 10 contributors.
- [Paperless Post](https://www.paperlesspost.com) — designed cards and invitations, the closest thing to a physical card in feel. Free cards available.
- [GroupGreeting](https://www.groupgreeting.com) — one card, everyone signs. Paid, with preview before sending.
- [Evite](https://www.evite.com) — invitations with RSVP tracking. Free with ads.

## Collaborative cards

When the gift is that twenty people remembered.

- [Kudoboard](https://www.kudoboard.com) — best-known option. Boards stay open indefinitely on paid plans.
- [GroupGreeting](https://www.groupgreeting.com) — corporate default. Scheduled delivery.
- [Thankbox](https://www.thankbox.com) — built for work goodbyes and retirements. Free for small groups.
- [Mila](https://www.mila.com) — European alternative, group cards and small gifts in one flow.

## Audio and voice

Underused, and the one people replay.

- [Vocaroo](https://vocaroo.com) — record and share a voice clip with a link. No account, files expire.
- [Whisper](https://whisper.sh) — anonymous confessions, not a gift tool, but people screenshot and send them.
- [Gifts QR voice wall](https://giftsqr.com) — collects 20-second recordings from multiple people and plays them in sequence behind one QR.
- [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai) — transcribe a long voice note so it reads as a letter.

## Video

- [Cameo](https://www.cameo.com) — a stranger with a specific fame records a message. Paid, price varies wildly.
- [Tribute](https://www.tribute.co) — collects video clips from a group, stitches them into one montage. Paid.
- [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com) — free browser editor if you're cutting the montage yourself.
- [Vidyard](https://www.vidyard.com) — screen + camera recording with view analytics, free tier. Useful for a "watch me open it" reply.

## Photography and albums

- [Google Photos shared album](https://photos.google.com) — unlimited-ish, zero friction, comments enabled.
- [Ente](https://ente.io) — end-to-end encrypted photo sharing, open source. Free tier 5GB.
- [Albumlink](https://album.link) — not photos; turns a set of songs into one shareable link. For the mixtape crowd.
- [Gifts QR event album](https://giftsqr.com) — guests scan one QR and upload straight into a shared album, no app.

## Time capsules and scheduled delivery

The gift arrives later, on purpose.

- [FutureMe](https://www.futureme.org) — email to yourself or someone else, delivered on a chosen date. Free, been running since 2002.
- [LetterMeLater](https://www.lettermelater.com) — same idea, supports attachments. Free tier.
- [Gifts QR time capsule](https://giftsqr.com) — sealed page with photos, letter and video that refuses to open before the date you set.

## Games and quizzes

- [Kahoot](https://kahoot.com) — build a quiz about the two of you, play it live on a call.
- [Skribbl.io](https://skribbl.io) — draw and guess, free, no account.
- [Gifts QR couple's trial](https://giftsqr.com) — exam about the relationship with a certificate at the end.

## Open source templates

Build it yourself if you'd rather own the file.

- [gift-page-templates](https://github.com/sostenesapollo/gift-page-templates) — three single-file HTML gift pages (heart burst, star sky, countdown). MIT, no build step.
- [canvas-confetti](https://github.com/catdad/canvas-confetti) — the confetti library behind half the celebration pages on the web.
- [qr-code-styling](https://github.com/kozakdenys/qr-code-styling) — styled QR generation with logo and gradients, browser and Node.
- [howler.js](https://github.com/goldfire/howler.js) — audio playback that behaves on mobile, which is where every gift page lives.

## Make it physical

The part that makes it land.

- Print the QR. A link in a chat is a link. The same link on paper inside a folded card becomes an object.
- Use [qr-code-styling](https://github.com/kozakdenys/qr-code-styling) to put a small photo in the center so it doesn't look like a parking ticket.
- Test the scan from a printed proof before you commit. Low contrast and small sizes fail indoors.
- Tuck it somewhere: inside a book, behind a photo, in a coat pocket.

## Rules of thumb

Learned from 50,000+ gifts created:

1. **Under 3 minutes to make, or it never gets sent.** If creation takes longer, the gift dies in a draft folder.
2. **One reaction beats ten features.** A single moment that lands is worth more than a page full of widgets.
3. **No login for the recipient.** Every gate between the tap and the gift loses people.
4. **Something has to move.** Static pages get a "aww" and a closed tab.
5. **The physical part is the gift.** The page is the payload; the paper is the delivery.
